About Heather

Clinically trained, and not squeamish about the rest

I’ve spent my career where trauma and addiction overlap, drawing on EMDR, hypnotherapy, and an IFS-informed, somatic approach. Most of what my clients carry has never been only a thinking problem, so we don’t treat it like one.

I work with adults, one to one, in person. If energy work, spiritual practice, or the felt sense of your own body is part of how you understand healing, you won’t have to translate that here.
